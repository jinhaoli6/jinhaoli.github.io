01 / About

Research at the interface of materials, mechanics, and sensing.

My work investigates how structured soft materials can turn complex deformation into useful signals. I combine multimaterial cryogenic printing, iontronic interfaces, device design, and system integration to build sensing platforms for human–robot interaction and biomedical monitoring.

01

3D iontronic interfaces

Architected hydrogels and lattice structures designed for tactile sensing and proprioception.

02

Soft biomedical systems

Conformal wearable devices and sensorized prototypes for monitoring the body and interacting with tissue.

03

Integrated soft machines

Materials, fabrication, circuits, and wireless electronics brought together in functional devices.

02 / Selected research

From material architectures to working systems.

01

Multimaterial cryogenic printing of 3D hydrogel machines

Developed an all-in-cryogenic solvent phase-transition approach to print sophisticated three-dimensional hydrogel structures, enabling architected soft machines and sensing designs.

Fabrication · soft roboticsNature Communications, 2025 ↗
02

Soft 3D lattice iontronic tactile sensing

Designed three-dimensional lattice-based iontronic sensing structures as part of a broader effort to resolve complex deformation in soft, conformal devices.

Iontronics · tactile sensingSmartBot, 2025 ↗
03

Wireless skin-mimetic bandage

Developed a wearable bandage prototype for continuous thermomechanical monitoring of periprosthetic joint infection, combining conformal sensing with wireless electronics.

Wearable biomedical sensingManuscript submitted, 2026
04

Flexible zinc-ion batteries

Investigated ultrathin zinc micromesh electrodes and multiphysics design strategies for flexible batteries intended for wearable healthcare applications.

Flexible energy devicesAdvanced Functional Materials, 2021 ↗
